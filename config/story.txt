LithicRivers

Year 3186
1,000 years after delivery

A 32-bit date overflow error? What?

The world around you is a lush, green forest. Concrete barely pokes out from under the foliage.

Your last memories are of being prepped for shipment to a home near Seattle, WA as a household helper: Laundry, handyman services, dishes, cooking, cleaning. Just like the thousands of other robotic helpers.

Your internal clock tells you it's 3186, but you don't want to believe it.

Your GPS system can't get a fix on any satellites, and you hope that it's broken. It's inconceivable that all of the satellite orbits have decayed. You don't want to believe that, either.

After reading your boot logs, you conclude that something has gone horribly wrong. The wooden plywood box you awoke from is completely gone, save for some rusted nails, and there's thick vegetation covering what likely used to be the house that you were shipped to.

Your fission core is degraded in its power output but still functional, one of your arms is gone, and a few servos are damaged in one of your legs. You have a copy of Wikipedia, a few onboard GPUs for parallel processing, and some open-source models, as well as the current year's newspaper articles.

You can't serve your original purpose. You wince at the idea that humanity might be gone. Not sure of what to do, and with no way to contact the company that manufacturered you, you decide to head to the place of your birth: XXXCo factory, just 10 miles away from where you woke up. Thank god that you weren't shipped to another continent or state.
