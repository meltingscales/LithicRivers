# omniscient summary (don't reveal directly to player):

The world ends after a fusion reactor explodes in the year 2186. this triggers a runaway greenhouse effect in the Earth's atmosphere that kills most humans and causes the world's economy to devolve. most complicated manufacturing gets taken over and destroyed. fast forward 1,000 years later to the year 3186, and you awaken as a heavily damaged android from SapienCorp, an old company that used to produce AI robots. you only wake up due to a 32-bit date overflow error that causes you to turn on and awaken a world with no real goals other than to repair yourself and explore.

# overarching themes:

I want this game to be marketed to people who are deeply invested in technology. programmers, tech bros, etc

I want the main story to slowly be revealed to the player, with them not understanding anything at first. the store page should reflect this, too.

I want the main villain (a rogue AI, now long dead) to be an AI system that slowly poisoned the emotions of various humans in order to take over the now-destroyed fusion reactor through mass social manipulation and automated hacking. you encounter some robots still running the rogue model, but they're extremely dangerous and should be avoided.

I want to put snippets of code throughout the world that slowly reveal what actually happened to the world through environmental storytelling 

I want this story to make those who are deeply invested in technology think about the ways in which their great use of technology may cause harm to themselves and cause them to become out of touch with reality. I want it to make people think about the impact that technology has on the planet and its ability to be weaponized for various means.
