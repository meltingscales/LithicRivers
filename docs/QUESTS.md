# Quests

## How the story is told

- **Slowly:** the player uncovers what happened piece by piece. The SapienCorp reactor explosion, what caused it, and the nuclear holocaust that followed are pieced together from quests, ruins, dialogue and item descriptions. No screen explains it up front.
- **The endgame goal is a secret:** the player's real purpose (building a strange biomechanical teleporter, not a rocket ship) is unknown to them until they have completed many quests.

## 1: Tutorial Quest

- spawn in the dark
- teaches you movement, torch mechanics
- first NPC urges you to use the "Tutorial" panel
  - ...and how to disable it
- get a diamond and a ScrapElectronics to the broken android to repair its power core, it fears it'll go out in a few months

## 2: A New Arm

- the repaired android (SC-M4X7) sends the robot to the SapienCorp facility half a mile north
- fight through the dogs, find the salvaged arm in the parts locker on the assembly line, install it in the Body tab

## 3: Black Box

- once the arm is in, SC-M4X7 points to SapienCorp's datacenter in a ruined city to the west
- the datacenter is dark: fuel its generator so the sealed door to the server hall has power, past the security androids
- bring the data shard back to SC-M4X7: a standard fusion core and a gun picked by the world seed
- **reveal:** the reactor was held past its limit for 41 days on orders from Chief Executive Odessa Crane and the board, for an unnamed project run by Dr. Emeric Thale ("Hold output. The aperture is almost stable."); SC-M4X7 remembers a sealed site under the river of fire, the hook for quest 4
