# Steam Store page content

## Short Description

An android wakes up in a world past its time. An ASCII sandbox where your physical body is the core crafting system, and every seed is reproducible down to the tick.

## Long Description

LithicRivers is a deterministic roguelike sandbox that blends Minecraft’s blocks, Factorio’s automation, and Qud’s body mutations, in pure ASCII. 

Mine, build, upgrade your broken android body, and explore infinite worlds of stone, factories, lava, and ruins.

- Deterministic Sandbox: Every action, drop, and dungeon is seeded and reproducible. Share a world and replay it exactly, tick by tick.

- Your body is your progression: Start as a damaged android. Repair, replace, and augment limbs to survive and become more capable.

- ASCII Worldbuilding: A survival sandbox in retro ASCII, with sprites from 1×1 blocks to 12×8 detailed art.

- Exploration: Carve through biomes above and below: wastelands, frozen springs, molten Lithic Rivers, and abandoned factories.

- Harness crafting, environmental effects, conveyor belts, and machines; discover how systems interact in unexpected, deterministic ways.
