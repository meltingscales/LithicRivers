Art in LithicRivers is all ASCII.

All blocks and items have 1x1, 2x2, 3x3 art sprites that can get rendered in the world.

In addition, there are 12x8 art sprites for detailed viewing. This is because most monospace fonts get rendered slightly taller than they are wide, so 12x8 is a good size for detailed art that looks roughly square.

I'm thinking of adding larger portraits for dialogue or large static art for specific scenes in the game, but haven't started that yet.
