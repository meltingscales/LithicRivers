# Design

## Context

- **Depth bands and a per-chunk feature stage** (`overhaul-worldgen-depth`): pure terrain per tile (`underground_tile`), then deposits and natural stairways. The survey test pins each band's mix.
- **The deep band** is the Lithic Rivers caverns, generated by the legacy terrain code.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Unmistakably different terrain.
- Rare, but findable with the compass.
- Deterministic, and cheap enough to generate per tile.

**Non-Goals:**
- New creatures.
- Processing machines (`add-advanced-processing`).
- Visual effects beyond the color shift.

## Decisions

### Placement
- **Regions:** tears are regions of 4 by 4 chunks. Each region's chance comes from `feature_rng(region, Feature::FractalTear)` (about 1 in 60). Regions within 2,000 tiles of the origin never roll.
- **Rifts:** a tear region in the deep can also open up to 3 layers into the shallows above it, shrinking each layer.

### Shapes
- **Mandelbrot or Julia, by region:** coordinates are mapped into the set's plane (the region spans about -2 to 1 on the real axis). A tile is open where the escape-time count passes a threshold, so the set's boundary becomes cavern walls, with tear glass on the exact boundary.
  - The iteration count is capped (64), and math is in f64 on fixed inputs, so the result is the same on every platform the game ships for. A test pins sample tiles.
- **Menger lattice:** a tile is wall unless, at some level, its base-3 digits in x and y are both 1 (the classic sponge test). The layer z picks which levels apply, so the lattice changes from layer to layer.
- **Bifurcation seams:** the logistic map's attractor at r chosen per region, drawn as seams of petrogen shale and uranic crust.

### Blocks
- Tear glass: solid, see-through for light, can't be mined.
- The other three are solid and mineable, with drops of their own items.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Floating-point differences across platforms] → The inputs are integers scaled by constants, the cap is low, and a test pins the results. If a platform ever differs, switch to fixed-point.
- [Too rare to find] → The biome compass, and a rift into the shallows.
