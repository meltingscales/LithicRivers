# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Clicking the remaining menus
The body repair window (its recipe list and its body-part list), the Quests list, the Inventory tab's list and tutorial selection SHALL record click areas for their rows; a click SHALL select a row, and clicking the selected row again SHALL do what Enter does.

#### Scenario: Repair by clicking
- **WHEN** the body repair window is open and the player clicks a recipe, then a damaged part
- **THEN** that recipe and that part are selected, as with the keys
