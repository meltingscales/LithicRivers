# Proposal

## Why

The Steam MVP asks for "basic sound effects, not just music" (`docs/STEAM-MVP.md`). Today the game plays only the opening music. A wood-crack effect is registered at startup but nothing ever plays it, so even the "mining action with SFX" item that's ticked in the MVP list is silent. Sound is a large part of how fast and physical mining, building and fighting feel (the ULTRAKILL and Downwell references in `docs/INSPIRATION.md`).

## What Changes

- **Sound cues from the game:** the core queues a sound cue when something audible happens. The client plays the queue each frame through the existing sound-effect channel and volume slider. Cues are not saved and don't affect the simulation, so runs stay deterministic.
- **Eight cues:**
  - Chop: the player fells a tree (the existing wood-crack sound).
  - Mine: the player mines rock, ore or a block.
  - Place: the player places a block or machine.
  - Pick up: items go into the player's inventory from the ground.
  - Hit: the player damages an enemy.
  - Hurt: the player takes damage.
  - Teleport: a jump.
  - Craft: a recipe is crafted.
- **Original sounds:** the seven new sounds are short WAV files generated by a committed script (`scripts/generate_sfx.py`) from seeded noise and tones, so they are LithicRivers' own and can be regenerated or tuned.
- **No pile-up:** a cue is dropped when the effect channel already has a few sounds waiting, so fast mining doesn't build a backlog of sounds that play late.

## Impact

- **Core:** `sound::SoundCue`, a non-saved `Resources::sounds` queue with `Resources::play`, and calls at player mining, block breaking, pickup, combat damage and teleporting.
- **Client:** registers the eight effects, plays queued cues in `step`, and queues Place and Craft where the client does those actions. `AudioManager::play_sound_effect` skips when the channel is backed up.
- **Assets:** seven WAV files (a few KB each) and the generator script.
